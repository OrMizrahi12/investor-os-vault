# Section 232 polysilicon tariff

*Written 2026-11-03. Each link shows the source and its date. "Calc." marks my arithmetic. Map: `catalysts.html`.*

## What it is
A catalyst for [[Corning]]. Type: Regulation. Could help (my reading).

**Evidence:**

- Proclamation dated Aug. 6, 2026. No fixed review date, and the floors can be changed "from time to time" ([Proclamation, Aug. 6, 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/adjusting-imports-of-polysilicon-and-its-derivatives-into-the-united-states/)).

## When
Dec. 4, 2026, 12:01 a.m. ET ([Proclamation, Aug. 6, 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/adjusting-imports-of-polysilicon-and-its-derivatives-into-the-united-states/)). Stockpiling limits run Sept. 22 to Dec. 3 ([Commerce rule, Federal Register, Sept. 24, 2026, search copy, page not opened](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/24/2026-19537/measures-to-restrict-stockpiling-of-polysilicon-and-polysilicon-derivatives-under-proclamation-11052)).

## Rough size
15% duty plus floors of $21/kg polysilicon, $100/kg ingots and wafers, $0.22/W cells, $0.38/W modules ([Proclamation, Aug. 6, 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/adjusting-imports-of-polysilicon-and-its-derivatives-into-the-united-states/)). Effect on Corning not sized.

## Already in the reported numbers
**None yet** (my reading). It takes effect after every reported period. Corning says it "improves the overall pricing environment and demand" ([Q3 release, Oct. 27, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000024741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm)), with no figure.

## If it happens
US-made polysilicon, wafers and modules get a price floor. Watch whether Solar margin moves in the first half of 2027. Until it pays off, it is strategic value and never counts toward covering the price. No thesis is written on Corning yet.

## If it does not happen
Waivers or changed floors would weaken it. Companies with approved plans can import without these duties during construction ([Proclamation, Aug. 6, 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/adjusting-imports-of-polysilicon-and-its-derivatives-into-the-united-states/)).
