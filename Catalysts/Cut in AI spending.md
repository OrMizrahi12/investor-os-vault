# Cut in AI spending

*Written 2026-11-03. Each link shows the source and its date. "Calc." marks my arithmetic. Lesson: `overlap.html`.*

## What it is
The dated test of kill line K9 for [[Corning]], claim C1: fiber stays short through 2028. It is the one event the paper portfolio on `overlap.html` shares across [[Corning]], [[Amphenol]], [[Broadcom]] and [[NVIDIA]]. The owner asked for this note and a line on Nov. 3, 2026. The measure and threshold are Claude's, for the owner to correct. No source in the vault says a cut is coming.

**The measure:** the filed cash flow lines for [[Meta]], [[Amazon]] and [[Microsoft]], added up for each calendar quarter. They are "Purchases of property and equipment" and, for Microsoft, "Additions to property and equipment". These are the three big data center buyers with typed links to Corning. A Q4 is the full year less nine months (calc.).

**The line:** lower than the quarter before, two quarters running, in any quarters through Q4 2030.

**Who else it reaches, for information:** Data Center was 92.5% of NVIDIA's revenue (calc., [10-Q, filed Aug. 26, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)). Broadcom's firm orders of about $179.2B include custom AI accelerators ([10-Q, filed Sept. 10, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000080/avgo-20260802.htm)). Amphenol reports "particular strength" in AI-related applications ([10-Q, filed Oct. 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926120646/aph-20260930x10q.htm)). Those three have no written thesis or kill lines, so this note cannot yet say it would break theirs.

## If it does not happen
It proves little. This is the demand side only: C1 also needs supply to stay short, and rival plants are the other half (see [[Rival fiber capacity from 2028]]).

**What the line can miss:**

- Capital spending is not all AI. Amazon's includes more than technology infrastructure, and no filing read splits out AI.
- It leaves out finance leases, which Meta and Microsoft use ([Meta 10-Q, filed Oct. 22, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026066773/meta-20260930.htm); [Microsoft 10-Q, filed Oct. 28, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436133/msft-20260930.htm)).
- It leaves out buyers with no note here, such as Alphabet. It also leaves out spending paid for by others, such as the financial partners Broadcom describes for AI labs ([10-Q, filed Sept. 10, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000080/avgo-20260802.htm)).
- It is late. Two falls take six months, and the filings follow by about a month.
