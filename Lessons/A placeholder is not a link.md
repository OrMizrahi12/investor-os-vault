# A placeholder is not a link

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught these while typing the links for `graph.html`. Ten notes said "Rival or supplier in the fiber chain of [[Corning]]", the same line whatever the company. [[Linde]] and [[Umicore]] said that while also saying no public link to Corning was found, and no source read ties [[STL]] to Corning. [[Furukawa Electric]], [[YOFC]] and [[Hengtong]] carried it though Corning's list of principal rivals leaves them out ([10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000124/glw-20251231.htm)).

[[Samsung Display share sale]] called Samsung Display "a customer and a holder". The filings read show a holder. No source read calls it a customer.

[[Amphenol]] said CommScope could not get glass from Corning. The article says "Corning ceasing glass sales to CommScope and others", from two unnamed ISPs ([Light Reading, Mar. 26, 2026](https://www.lightreading.com/fttx/bead-winners-fret-over-fiber-costs-lead-times)). So that link may have ended. No decision used any of these.

## What it taught us

A line that fits every company says nothing about any of them. Each link needs its own source, and a company with no source gets no link.

## What changed

The ten notes now say what each link rests on, and each has a "Typed links" section. [[Samsung Display share sale]] and [[Amphenol]] are fixed. No rule changed.
