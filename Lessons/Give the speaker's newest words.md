# Give the speaker's newest words

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught this checking the skeptic's first review. Its top objection quoted Corning's CEO, "there is enough fiber in the world to meet demand" ([Lightwave, Jan. 29, 2026, read through a summarizing fetch](https://lightwaveonline.com/home/article/55354067/cornings-ceo-says-theres-enough-fiber-in-the-world-to-meet-demand)). It listed his current view as an open question. On the Q3 call he said "Things are tight" ([Q3 call, Oct. 27, 2026, third-party transcript, read through a summarizing fetch](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/27/corning-glw-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)), and an earlier note in the vault already carried that. The reviewer read that call for other quotes.

## What it taught us

An old quote can make a stronger objection than the facts allow. Before leaning on one, look for the same speaker's newest words on the point, and give both with their dates.
