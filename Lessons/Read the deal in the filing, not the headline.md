# Read the deal in the filing, not the headline

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught these while testing the claims in [[Corning]] for `edge.html`. [[T1 Energy]] said T1 "buys" polysilicon and wafers, from trade press. Its own filing says the wafer agreement "is subject to certain conditions" ([T1 Energy 8-K, Aug. 15, 2025][t1]). [[NVIDIA]] said a $296M warrant offsets the deposit. The filed sum uses $267M, the long-term portion, and the net rounds the same ([10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026][q]). Neither reached a decision.

T1's CEO also said on its call of Aug. 12, 2026, "we buy all of our wafers from Corning or Hemlock Semiconductor" ([Q2 call, third-party transcript](https://www.webull.com/news/15429873268204544)). So "buys" is T1's own word too. It still does not show that the conditions were met or that shipments began.

## What it taught us

Trade press drops conditions. Read the party's own filing for a deal's terms, and keep a conditional deal conditional until a source says it cleared.
