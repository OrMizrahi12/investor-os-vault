# Check counts and ranges in the words too

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught three slips while building `priced-in.html`, all before handover. Rereading the draft found that operating cash flow reached 24.8% of sales in "three" of ten years, when it was two, FY2016 and FY2018 (calc.). The separate check script's printout showed that growth swings "11 to 22" points down a table column, when it is 19 to 22 (calc.). Claude also wrote a link to the wrong 8-K as a stand-in for the share program, and replaced it with the [8-K, filed Sept. 11, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000119312526389321/d154232d8k.htm). No decision used any of them.

## What it taught us

[[Compute the numbers in the words]] checked single figures. A count ("three of ten") or a range ("11 to 22") summarizes many figures, and it is easy to write from a glance. Print each one from the script. [[A placeholder is not a link]] still applies: pick the real source while writing.

## What changed

The three are fixed on `priced-in.html`. No rule changed.
