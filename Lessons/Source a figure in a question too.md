# Source a figure in a question too

*Written 2026-11-04.*

## What happened

Claude caught this. In a question on how big a paper fall I could sit through, Claude's option said the S&P 500 fell about 50% in 2000-02 and 2007-09, "from memory, not checked". The saved file gives 41.6% from Aug. 2000 to Feb. 2003 and 49.0% from Oct. 2007 to Mar. 2009 (calc., monthly averages with dividends, [Shiller, file dated Oct. 6, 2026, fetched Nov. 3, 2026](https://shillerdata.com/)). I had already answered 35% when it was checked. The loose figure may have colored that answer, and we cannot tell.

## What it taught us

A figure offered as context for my answer is still a fact. Labeling it "from memory" does not meet `Rules/facts.md`. Look it up before asking, since the file was already in the vault.

## What changed

Claude corrected the figure in the same session, and `constitution-mine.html` uses the file's figures. No rule changed.
