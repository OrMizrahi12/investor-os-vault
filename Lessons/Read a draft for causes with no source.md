# Read a draft for causes with no source

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught this reading the draft of `cash-reserve.html`, before showing it. Every number had a source and a check. Two plain sentences did not. One said earnings rules, buybacks and the mix of companies had all changed. The other called the U.S. market one of the best on record. Both came from memory. The figure checks passed, because a check on numbers cannot see a sentence with no number in it. No decision used them.

## What it taught us

A sentence that explains a number is a fact too. Read the words on their own for claims that have no source, above all sentences giving a cause.

## What changed

The page now cites [Shiller's data page, undated, retrieved Nov. 3, 2026](http://www.econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data.htm), for buybacks only, and the U.S. claim is gone. No rule changed.
