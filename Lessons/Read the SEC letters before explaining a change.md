# Read the SEC letters before explaining a change

*Written 2026-11-03.*

## What happened

Claude caught this while running the red flags. [[Corning]] recorded the April 2026 change in core as a plain method change. It missed that SEC staff had told Corning to remove the old adjustment ([SEC staff letter, Feb. 26, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000000000026002022/filename1.pdf)). The letters were on the EDGAR list we drew on Nov. 2. They were counted and not read. No decision used the incomplete note.

## What it taught us

UPLOAD and CORRESP filings are where the staff and the company argue about the accounts. Company releases may leave that out. When a method changes, look there for the reason.

## What changed

[[Corning]] now records the letters. `flags.html` rates this as a concern. No rule changed.
