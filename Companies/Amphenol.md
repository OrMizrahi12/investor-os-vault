# Amphenol

*Written 2026-11-03. Each link shows the source and its date. Public evidence, not firsthand. Ticker and sector were not checked. Side by side: `rivals.html`.*

## What it is

US connector maker that bought CommScope's connectivity business. Corning names it a principal Optical rival.

## Relationship

Rival of [[Corning]] in Optical, named in its 10-K. Through CommScope's connectivity business, which it bought in Jan. 2026, it is also reported as a buyer of Corning glass, and those sales may have ended. See "Typed links" below. Also see "Rivals and suppliers" in [[Corning]].

## Terms

- Communications Solutions, Q3 2026: $6,583.0M, up 99%, all products. CommScope forecast at $5.1B for 2026 ([Amphenol Q3 release, Oct. 28, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000820313/000110465926119421/aph-ex99d1.htm)).
- Communications networks sales fell 28% organically on "moderations in demand from the broadband market" ([Amphenol Q3 call, Oct. 28, 2026, third-party transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/amphenol-aph-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).
- Materials are "challenging" to get, "including glass". CommScope has acted to secure supply. No fiber capacity figure given ([Amphenol Q3 call, Oct. 28, 2026, third-party transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/amphenol-aph-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).

- Two ISPs said CommScope canceled their orders after it could not get Buy America glass from Corning ([Light Reading, Mar. 26, 2026](https://www.lightreading.com/fttx/bead-winners-fret-over-fiber-costs-lead-times)). Whether it also makes its own glass was not found. The article's words, from those ISPs: "Corning ceasing glass sales to CommScope and others". Corning said it would not comment on specific dialogues (same source).
- **Split (added Nov. 3, 2026):** two-for-one, shares distributed Sept. 2, 2026, and prior periods adjusted in the [10-Q, filed Oct. 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926120646/aph-20260930x10q.htm). FY2025 EPS of $3.34 in the [10-K, filed Feb. 11, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926013549/aph-20251231x10k.htm) is on the old shares.
- **Twelve months to Sept. 30, 2026 (added Nov. 3, 2026, for `multiples.html`):** diluted EPS $2.30, operating income $9,160M, operating cash flow less capex $5,121M (calc., [10-Q, filed Oct. 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926120646/aph-20260930x10q.htm)). The CommScope purchase closed Jan. 9, 2026, so these hold about nine months of it, and the debt holds all of it.
- **End market (added Nov. 3, 2026, for `overlap.html`):** IT datacom was about 36% of 2025 net sales, communications networks about 10%, industrial 19%, automotive 15%, defense 9%, mobile devices 6% and commercial aerospace 5%. No single customer was 10% or more in 2023 to 2025 ([10-K, filed Feb. 11, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926013549/aph-20251231x10k.htm)). That is before CommScope. In Q3 2026, Communications Solutions was about 64% of net sales, with "outsized" organic IT datacom growth and "particular strength" in AI-related applications. The 10-Q gives no current IT datacom share ([10-Q, filed Oct. 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926120646/aph-20260930x10q.htm)).
