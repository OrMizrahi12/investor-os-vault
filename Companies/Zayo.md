# Zayo

*Written 2026-11-02. Each link shows the source and its date.*

## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

Zayo is a fiber network company serving carriers, cloud providers, data centers, schools and businesses. DigitalBridge and EQT acquired it in 2020, and it is a DigitalBridge portfolio company ([Zayo and DigitalBridge release, Mar. 13, 2025][dbr]).

**Ticker:** blank because I read that ownership to mean it is private. The source is from March 2025, and I did not find a later change.

**Sector:** unknown. I found no GICS sector for it.

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Customer of [[Corning]], under an "expanded strategic supply agreement" for optical fiber cable ([joint release, reprinted by TelecomTV, Aug. 21, 2026][ttv]).

## Terms
%% Deal size, length and dates. If they are not disclosed, say unknown. %%

- **Date:** Aug. 20, 2026 ([Broadband Breakfast, Aug. 20, 2026][bbz]). The date of the earlier agreement it expands is unknown.
- **Size:** unknown. The companies declined to disclose financial terms ([Broadband Breakfast, Aug. 20, 2026][bbz]).
- **Length:** covers "a significant portion" of the fiber Zayo projects it will need through the rest of the decade ([TelecomTV, Aug. 21, 2026][ttv]).
- **Zayo's plan:** add 15,000 route miles by 2030, including more than 8,000 long-haul miles with NVIDIA ([TelecomTV, Aug. 21, 2026][ttv]). This is a target, not a result.
