# Apple

## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

Apple is listed under GICS as Information Technology, Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals, ticker AAPL ([Wikipedia S&P 500 list, undated, retrieved Nov. 2, 2026][wiki]). This is a reference list, not S&P or MSCI, which publish GICS. It sells the iPhone and Apple Watch ([WBKO, Aug. 6, 2025][wbko]).

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

## Terms

## Why it matters
%% What this relationship means for the company's thesis, and what happens if it ends. %%

The thesis on [[Corning]] is not written yet, so what this means for it is still open. Corning's CEO called the commitment "a larger, longer-term opportunity" ([Q3 2025 release, Oct. 28, 2025][q325]). It appears in an earnings release but is not traceable in the numbers ([deals page, Nov. 2, 2026](../corning-deals-vs-prepayments.html)).

If it ends: a whole plant is dedicated to one customer, so that plant would be exposed. The sales effect is unknown, because Apple's share of Corning's sales is not disclosed. Until the deal has paid off, it is strategic value and never counts toward covering the price ([[when-to-buy]]).
