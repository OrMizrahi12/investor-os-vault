## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

Heliene makes solar modules. Its headquarters is in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, and it has factories in Mountain Iron and Rogers, Minnesota ([KAXE, Aug. 6, 2026][kaxe]).

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Partner of [[Corning]] in a US solar supply chain, with [[Suniva]]. Corning supplies wafers and Hemlock supplies polysilicon. Suniva makes the cells and Heliene makes the modules at Mountain Iron ([Manufacturing Dive, Mar. 11, 2025][md]).
