## Terms
%% Deal size, length and dates. If they are not disclosed, say unknown. %%

- **Date:** announced in mid-May 2025. I did not confirm the exact day ([RCR Wireless, May 16, 2025][rcr]).
- **Length:** a Broadcom executive called it "a multi-year collaboration". No end date is given ([RCR Wireless, May 16, 2025][rcr]).
- **Size:** unknown. No dollar value is given ([RCR Wireless, May 16, 2025][rcr]).
- **End market (added Nov. 3, 2026, for `overlap.html`):** Semiconductor solutions were 70% of revenue in the quarter ended Aug. 2, 2026. The 10-Q gives no AI share of revenue. Firmly committed remaining obligations were about $179.2B, including a long-term contract for custom AI accelerators, with about 25% expected within 12 months ([10-Q, filed Sept. 10, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016826000080/avgo-20260802.htm)).
- **Customers (added Nov. 3, 2026):** the top five were about 50% of revenue for the three fiscal quarters, not named (same 10-Q).
- **Help for customers (added Nov. 3, 2026):** a lease backstop of about $29B, undiscounted, once all racks are deployed, and possible customer convertible notes of up to $42B. Its words: deploying AI infrastructure "requires our customers to access significant capital" (same 10-Q).
- **Suppliers (added Nov. 3, 2026):** about 95% of contract-made wafers came from TSMC in the three fiscal quarters (same 10-Q).
