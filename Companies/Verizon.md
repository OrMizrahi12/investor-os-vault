## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

Verizon is listed under GICS as Communication Services, Integrated Telecommunication Services, ticker VZ ([Wikipedia S&P 500 list, undated, retrieved Nov. 2, 2026][wiki]). This is a reference list, not S&P or MSCI, which publish GICS. It aims to reach 40 to 50 million fiber locations over the medium term ([Light Reading, Sept. 8, 2026][lr]).
