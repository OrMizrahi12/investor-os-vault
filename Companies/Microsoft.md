# Microsoft

*Written 2026-11-02. Each link shows the source and its date.*

## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

Microsoft is listed under GICS as Information Technology, Systems Software, ticker MSFT ([Wikipedia S&P 500 list, undated, retrieved Nov. 2, 2026][wiki]). This is a reference list, not S&P or MSCI, which publish GICS. It is ramping up hollow-core fiber in its networks ([Fierce Network, Sept. 24, 2025][fierce]).

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Manufacturing partner of [[Corning]]. Corning's North Carolina factories are to make Microsoft's hollow-core fiber ([Fierce Network, Sept. 24, 2025][fierce]). Corning is not the only maker: Microsoft's own blog names "new strategic manufacturing collaborations" with Corning and Heraeus, under the title "Microsoft Azure scales Hollow Core Fiber (HCF) production through outsourced manufacturing". It calls the design "proprietary" ([Azure blog, Sept. 23, 2025, Exa's copy][msb]). A direct fetch returned only the title. Public evidence, not firsthand.
