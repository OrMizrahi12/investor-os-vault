# T1 Energy

*Written 2026-11-02. Each link shows the source and its date.*

## What it is
%% What this customer, supplier or partner does. %%

T1 Energy is a US solar manufacturer. Its cell plant in Austin, Texas was under construction when reported, and its modules are made in Dallas ([PV Tech, Aug. 15, 2025][pvt]).

**Ticker and sector:** TE on the NYSE, sector Industrials ([Snowball Analytics, undated, retrieved Nov. 2, 2026][snte]). Data sites disagree: Benzinga puts it in Information Technology ([Benzinga, undated, search excerpt only, Nov. 2, 2026][benz]). None of these is S&P or MSCI, which publish GICS.

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Customer of [[Corning]], under agreements for Hemlock polysilicon and Corning wafers. When signed, the wafer agreement was "subject to certain conditions", and the polysilicon agreement "is conditional upon the effectiveness of the Wafer Supply Agreement" ([T1 Energy 8-K, Aug. 15, 2025][t1k]). Whether the conditions were met is not stated in what I read. Whether shipments began is unknown.

**Its own words, public evidence, not firsthand:** "We buy all of our polysilicon and we buy all of our wafers from Corning or Hemlock Semiconductor." (CEO Daniel Barcelo.) "we're anchor customers for Hemlock Poly and Corning wafers." (Chief Legal and Policy Officer Andy Munro.) "we're covered on the Corning contract for wafer that relates to Phase 1." "Corning is a domestic sourced contract at a price." (CFO Evan Calio.) All from its [Q2 call, Aug. 12, 2026, third-party transcript][t1c].
