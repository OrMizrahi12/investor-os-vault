## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Corning plans to expand US connectivity capacity 10x and US fiber capacity by more than 50% ([Q2 release, July 28, 2026][q2]). Those are plans, not results.

## Terms
%% Deal size, length and dates. If they are not disclosed, say unknown. %%

- **Date:** May 6, 2026 ([8-K, May 6, 2026][8k]).
- **Contract value:** unknown. It is not disclosed in the sources I read.
- **Warrants:** NVIDIA paid $500 million for a warrant on up to 15 million shares at $180.00 and a pre-funded warrant on up to 3 million shares at $0.0001 ([8-K, May 6, 2026][8k]). The first would bring in about $2.7 billion if fully exercised (calc.: 15M × $180, [8-K, May 6, 2026][8k]).
- **Warrant length:** the $180 warrant ends at the earliest of three years from issue, termination of the partnership agreement (with exceptions) or a fundamental transaction ([8-K, May 6, 2026][8k]).
- **Deposit:** a customer paid a $1.0 billion deposit under a supply agreement through Dec. 31, 2029. $267 million, the long-term portion of a warrant, offsets it, for about $0.7 billion net. The warrant's full grant-date value is $296 million ([10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026][q0930]). The 10-Q says "a customer" and that the warrant went to that customer. That warrant has the same size and price as the one the 8-K says NVIDIA holds. So the deposit is NVIDIA's, confirmed by the two filings read together (my decision, Nov. 3, 2026). Neither filing states it in one sentence.
- **Its own words, public evidence, not firsthand:** NVIDIA's CEO said on CNBC that it made "a multi-billion-dollar prepayment" ([Reuters on Yahoo, May 8, 2026][nr]). That is more than the $1.0 billion deposit above, and neither source explains the gap. NVIDIA's own 10-Q does not name Corning in what I read, and calls its supply commitments "primarily memory and manufacturing facilities" ([10-Q, quarter ended July 26, 2026][nq]).
- **End market (added Nov. 3, 2026, for `overlap.html`):** Data Center revenue $89,023M of $96,221M in the quarter ended July 26, 2026, or 92.5% (calc.). Hyperscale $48,710M, or 50.6% (calc.). Three direct customers were 16%, 15% and 13% of first-half revenue, not named ([10-Q, filed Aug. 26, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)).
- **Help for customers (added Nov. 3, 2026):** commitments of $56B on AI cloud agreements and third-party data center leases, and $25B of equity commitments, including AI model makers and infrastructure financiers (same 10-Q).
- **Suppliers (added Nov. 3, 2026):** wafers from TSMC and Samsung, memory from SK Hynix, Micron and Samsung ([10-K, filed Feb. 25, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)).

## Why it matters

The thesis on [[Corning]] is not written yet, so what this means for it is still open. This is the only deal of the 13 timeline entries that can be traced into reported numbers, and that trace rests on the two filings above ([deals page, Nov. 2, 2026](../corning-deals-vs-prepayments.html)).
