## What it is

## Relationship
%% Customer, supplier or partner, and of which company. Link that company's note. %%

Partner of [[Corning]] in a US solar supply chain, with [[Heliene]]. Corning supplies wafers and Hemlock supplies polysilicon. Suniva makes the cells and Heliene makes the modules ([Manufacturing Dive, Mar. 11, 2025][md]).
