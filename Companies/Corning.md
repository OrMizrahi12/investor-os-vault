## What the company does
%% What it makes, who buys it, and how big it is. %%

Corning makes specialty glass, ceramics and related materials that other companies build into their products, including fiber-optic cable, display and phone glass, and car parts ([10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026][k]).

Its buyers include telecom operators, businesses and governments, display panel makers, mobile device makers, emission-control system makers, drug makers and labs, and solar and chip companies ([10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026][k]).

It had $15,629M in FY2025 sales, about 67,200 employees in 44 countries, and factories in 14 countries ([10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026][k]).

## Biggest customers

**Corning's annual report does not identify its largest customers, so no ranked list is available from it.** It does say that a few customers make up much of each segment's sales. Shares of 2025 segment sales, under the old segments, were: Optical 28% (2 customers), Display 59% (3), Specialty Materials 43% (2), Automotive 61% (3) and Life Sciences 45% (2) ([10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026][k]).
