## 4. Selection rules
- Defensive stock purchases must meet the seven defensive criteria (size, financial strength, 10-year earnings stability, 20-year dividend record, earnings growth, P/E ≤ 15× 3-yr average, P/B ≤ 1.5 or P/E×P/B ≤ 22.5), or be made through index funds.
- Enterprising purchases require a documented margin of safety: price ≤ two-thirds of conservatively appraised value, or price below net current assets (as a diversified group), or the enterprising screen.
- Bonds: high grade only; coverage per the coverage table; maturities matched to needs; no buying lower-grade issues at full price for yield.

## 5. Things I will not do
- [ ] Buy on margin or borrow to invest
- [ ] Buy IPOs or "hot" new issues
- [ ] Buy a stock because it has gone up, or sell because it has gone down
- [ ] Act on market forecasts, tips, or price targets
- [ ] Hold more than __% in my employer's stock
- [ ] Buy high-cost products (fund expenses above __%, annuities with surrender charges, structured products)
- [ ] Mix speculative and investment money

## 6. When selling is allowed
- The business's value has deteriorated (not merely its price), after re-analysis.
- The price has risen far above a conservative value (Mr. Market is offering a silly price).
- Rebalancing requires it.
- A planned need or emergency (health, job loss, home purchase, tuition).
- Tax-loss harvesting under current law.
