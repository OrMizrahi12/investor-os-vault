## My rules

The reasons are in `rules.md`, in the project root. If this list and that file ever differ, stop and ask me.

1. Hold for five years or more.
2. Keep 5 to 10 positions, up to 20 when there is good reason.
3. Long only: no short selling.
4. Always keep a cash reserve, so fear of missing out never forces a buy.
5. Size each position by how strong the thesis is and how much I lose if it is wrong.
6. Act on the thesis, not the price.
