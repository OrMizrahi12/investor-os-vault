## Overrides to the skills

Items 1 to 7 apply to `value-investing`, and to `field-research-investing` wherever it offers the same thing. Items 8 and 9 apply to `field-research-investing`.

1. **Name the lens: an enterprising investor, concentrated, on my rules.** Few positions, long only, five years or more. Do not default to the skill's defensive investor. Enterprising describes the work and the tests to read. It does not bring in the skill's bargain groups, net-nets or price tests.
2. **Tests are evidence, not gates.** A pass does not qualify a stock, and a fail does not bar it. Only `when-to-buy` and `when-to-trim` lead to action. Drop "must pass all seven", the waiver count and the skill's verdict. Recommendations follow `recommendations`.
3. **Run the bars unchanged, then read them.** For each fail, say whether it speaks about the company, the test or both. Count a deficit on reported earnings and show normalized beside it. Update dollar floors with the latest CPI. Show after-tax coverage for information only.
4. **No mix or buying schedule unless I ask.** No stock-bond band, ±5 rebalancing, dollar-cost averaging or "buy whenever you have money". The cash reserve rule applies.
5. **Price tests stay off except inside the method in `when-to-buy`.** No 22.5, price ceiling, 8.5 + 2g, net-net or bargain test, and no "price at which it qualifies" other than the buy-below price. A market-value size floor may run, because it uses the price as a size only.
6. **Positions and sizing are mine.** 5 to 10, up to 20, sized by thesis and loss. No 10 to 30, equal dollars, 90-10 index core or net-net groups.
7. **Selling is mine.** No rebalancing sale, "excessive advance" swap or scaling out. Only `when-to-trim` and a broken thesis. No speculative account or event bets.
8. **Three independent public customer sources replace interviews.** Where the skill asks for three key customers heard firsthand, use three public sources instead, each about a different customer. Independent means none comes from the company and none copies another. Label this as public evidence, not firsthand. Do not plan calls to people unless I ask.
9. **Short-side tools only spot red flags.** Warning phrases, count checks and the like can raise a flag or test a thesis. They never lead to a short candidate, a short thesis or a short decision.
