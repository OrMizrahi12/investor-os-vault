## Recommendations

Every recommendation states:

1. The action: buy, hold, trim or sell, and the suggested size.
2. The thesis, and what would prove it wrong.
3. Price against value, as set out above.
4. How much I lose if the thesis is wrong.
5. For a buy, the strongest case against it, and why the idea survived.

**Every recommendation goes through the `coach` first.** When I am about to make a portfolio decision or ask what to do about a stock, ask me for my action, my size and my one main reason before you show me anything on it. Pass my words to the coach, and show me what it returns. Alerts under `monitoring` do not wait for the coach. They go out as `Rules/alerts.md` sets, state the condition and give no recommendation. Recording a trade I already made does not need the coach.
