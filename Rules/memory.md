## Memory

**This vault is your memory. Read it at the start of a session and write to it at the end.** Keep research memory here, not in your private memory, so I can read and correct it.

### Where each type lives

| Memory type | Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Rules | `Rules/` | How I invest and what you may do. The reasons are in `rules.md`. |
| Knowledge | `Companies/` | What is true about each company and how companies connect. Company and entity notes go here. |
| Catalysts | `Catalysts/` | Events that could change how the market sees a company. One note per catalyst, linked to its company. |
| Journal | `Journal/` | What we did, decided and left open, by date. |
| Lessons | `Lessons/` | What experience taught us and what changed because of it. |
| Playbooks | `Playbooks/` | Step-by-step routines for jobs that repeat. |
| Portfolio | `Portfolio/` | What I own and how much cash I hold. The thesis stays in the company note. Never guess a holding. |
| Inbox | `Inbox/` | New items from the sensors, waiting to be read. One dated note per filing, not a view on the company, plus any idea notes from the monthly hunt: a lead to read, never a pick, with why it surfaced and its source and date. Checked facts move to the company note. |
| Research | `Research/` | Researcher reports waiting for me to merge. Not checked into company notes yet. |

`Templates/` holds the blank forms. It holds no memory.

### At the start of a session

1. Read the three latest Journal entries, going by file name.
2. For any company we discuss, read its note in `Companies/` before you answer, even if it comes up midway. Search `Journal/` and `Lessons/` for its name too. If it has no note, say so.

Notes are dated records. Check anything that may have changed before you rely on it. Open `Playbooks/` and `Portfolio/` when the work needs them.

### At the end of every working session

A working session is any session where we researched, decided or changed a file.

1. Write a Journal entry in `Journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, under three headings: **What we did**, **What we decided** and **What we left open**. Write "nothing" under a heading if that is the truth. A decision not to act counts.
2. For a decision, also give the reasons, what it does not say and what would reopen it. **For a decision on a stock, a pass included, also give a check: a date, and the dated fact that would show whether the reason held. When that date or a reopen event comes, add one entry: did the reason hold, judged only on what was on file at the decision, and what is the reason now? Put the result on its own line. One result alone is no ground to change a rule, a method, a grade or a chance.**
3. Write it when the work is done. Do not wait for me to say goodbye, because you get no signal when I close the session. If we carry on, update it.
4. If today's file already exists, add to the end of it. Never rewrite an earlier entry. If one is wrong, say so in a new entry.

New facts go in the company's note, each with its source and date, and the note's "last checked" is updated.

### Corrections

Put every correction in `Lessons/`, whether I caught the mistake or you did. Fix the wrong note too, and link it under "What changed".

### Sources

- Mark every source with its date, in every folder. Put the date in the link text, next to the fact: `[Q3 release, Oct. 27, 2026][q3]`.
- If a source has no date, write `undated, retrieved` and the day you read it.
- The `sources:` property is only a list of links. It does not replace the date next to the fact.

### What stays out

Possible inside tips stay out of every folder, including the Journal.
