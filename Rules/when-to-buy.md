## When to buy

- **Recommend a buy only when the proven base value covers at least 75% of the price.** Coverage is the middle of the base range ÷ the price. The buy-below price is that middle ÷ 0.75. Being under it is required, and it is not enough: a thesis and the skeptic's review still come first.
- Base value is what the business has already proven. Strategic value is what may come later, such as targets, forecasts and deals that have not paid off yet. It can add upside, but it never counts toward covering the price.
- **Base value is worked out this way, and only this way:**
  - **Earnings:** normal earnings on today's diluted shares. Low is the ten-year average, middle the last audited year, high the last twelve months.
  - **Cash:** free cash flow with customer deposits out. Growth forever at no more than the company's own record, and never above 3%.
  - **Votes:** 40% normal earnings ÷ 9%, 40% the cash sum at 9%, 20% normal earnings ÷ the Aaa yield.
  - **No vote, always shown:** past multiples, rival multiples and the growth the price needs. Targets, unshipped deals and annualized quarters never count.
  - **Refresh** after each 10-Q and 10-K. The 9% reopens if Aaa moves a point from 6.12%. Changing the method needs my approval. A new company needs its own check that the method fits.
- A rising price, a rumor or a good story is not value.
- Show the base value, the price and the share of the price covered, each with its source and date. If you cannot show them, do not recommend the buy.
- **Every buy idea must survive a skeptic's review, the strongest case against it, before you show it to me.**
- **An add is a new buy of the whole position.** It must clear the three gates in `sizing` on a value refreshed from the newest filing, the size and limits counted on the whole position, and the cash levels. It also needs a skeptic's review dated after my last buy, with my ruling that the idea survived, and my own reading of a report dated after my last buy, that the thesis holds. No Red or Orange may be waiting on the stock or its group. No schedule and no averaging in. A lower price passes gate 3 and nothing else.
