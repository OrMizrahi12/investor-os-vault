    How the walk works

    • It follows the event's chain. A fiber event travels fiber links only, so a solar or cover glass customer stays dark. "All chains" turns this off.
    • It runs both ways along a link. A customer's trouble reaches its supplier, and a supplier's trouble reaches its customers.
    • One step means a direct typed link. Two steps means through one company in between. Each company shows at its shortest distance, and extra routes are listed.
    • It does not size anything. Most shares of sales are not disclosed, so light means possible, not large. A weak link makes the whole path weak.
    • Click any company in Walk mode to start from it. "Any event" follows all of its links.
