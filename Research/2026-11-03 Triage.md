## The gates: Claude's choices, accepted by me on Nov. 3, 2026

**Gate 1, here and stronger in five years.** A forecast cannot be checked, so this tests the record. All must hold: profit in at least 8 of the last 10 fiscal years; 12-month free cash flow above zero; debt at most 4× a year's operating cash; operating profit at least 3× interest on average; sales above where the record starts. With under ten years on file it stays open, unless losses already rule out 8 of 10.

**Gate 2, base value covers the price.** `Rules/when-to-buy.md`, summed by `value.py`, with a shortcut: reported net income stands in for normal earnings, and cash is 12-month free cash flow less the 12-month change in contract liabilities, one reading. Corning uses its approved inputs. Past and rival multiples are unknown here, and strategic value is not worked out. Every other company's base is a proposal.

**Gate 3, a real edge.** Something true, material and not priced that we hold and most do not. Public filings read quickly do not count.

**Sorting.** A fail at Gate 1 or 2 is a reject. Passing or staying open through Gate 2 and failing Gate 3 is the watchlist. Passing all three is research. An open gate does not stop a company, and a later fail still does.

**Pick rule for the next triage, my decision, Nov. 3, 2026.** Rank leads by the rise against company size, not by the largest dollar rise. Claude's reading, for me to correct: the largest rise on one line ÷ the latest full year's revenue, from SEC data, with no price used. ## Micron: why Gate 2 is open

On the method, Micron clears the bar: 99.9% (calc.). The method takes the last audited year as the middle, and that year is far above any before it. Net income was $84,969M in FY2026, against $14,135M in FY2018, the best earlier year (calc., [10-K, filed Oct. 9, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm); [SEC company facts, retrieved Nov. 3, 2026](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000723125.json)).

- The base range's low covers 31.5% (calc.). That low still carries FY2026's cash in its cash vote.
- **What-if, not the method:** with FY2018's earnings and free cash flow as the middle, coverage is 17.2% (calc.).
- The $13B of contract liabilities is mainly customer deposits, and "Nearly all of the deposits are scheduled to be repaid between 2029 and 2031" ([10-K, filed Oct. 9, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm)). They are out of the cash figure.
- The $134B backlog behind the lead is "not expected to be indicative of future revenue under these contracts" ([10-K, filed Oct. 9, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm)).

**My ruling, Nov. 3, 2026: the method does not fit Micron on a peak year, and it stays on the watchlist.** The method was not changed. This is not a buy idea: it has no thesis, no skeptic's review and no edge.
