# Corning: fundamental, Nov. 3, 2026

Nothing was filed after the Oct. 29 10-Q, and I found no debt deal, correction or rating action. No classic test result changes. The new facts are all in that 10-Q and missing from the note. The largest is a change on foreign cash: Corning now expects to repatriate earnings, and a tax it calls "withholding" pushed the Q3 tax rate to 22.9%. A Korean tax judgment on Oct. 1 also goes to appeal. Not advice. Base value and value are not defined yet. The note has no thesis, so nothing here tests one.

**Korean tax**

- **filing, baseline.** A final ruling for 2010 to 2012 cost a $92M noncash tax charge in Q1 2026. - **calc.** $150M is 1.1% of Corning shareholders' equity of $13,671M and 22% of Q3 net income of $680M. This sizes the balance only. It is not a forecast of a charge ([10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000279/glw-20260930.htm)). It does not change the note's "What could wipe out shareholders" reading.
