# Corning: strategic, Nov. 3, 2026

Little is new since the Q3 release. I found no new deal and no officer or director change, and Corning's only filing since the release is the 10-Q. What is new: rivals' Q3 reports show price rising fast, a Korean tax judgment first disclosed in the 10-Q, and more detail on India. No thesis is written, so nothing here tests one. Base value and value are not defined yet.

### Catalysts and contract footprints

Each of these is strategic value and never counts toward covering the price.

**New: South Korean tax appeal** (filing, [10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000279/glw-20260930.htm), Note 11)

- **What it is:** Corning Precision Materials, a Korean subsidiary, is appealing tax assessments and had to deposit the disputed amounts. A final unfavorable ruling in 2026 on tax years 2010 to 2012 led to a $92M noncash charge. On Oct. 1, 2026 it received a judgment on "the remaining matters", which it intends to appeal. The deposit receivable is $150M, against $248M at Dec. 31, 2025.
- **When:** no date is given.
- **If it happens:** an appeal win would support the $150M receivable. Corning says it is "more likely than not" to prevail. That is its own forecast.
- **If it does not happen:** the earlier ruling suggests a similar noncash charge against the remaining balance, up to $150M (my reading). Whether the Oct. 1 judgment went against Corning is not said outright, though it plans to appeal.
- **Outcome:** not known on Nov. 3, 2026.
