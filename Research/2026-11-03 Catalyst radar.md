## Market screen: SEC XBRL frames

These are leads to read, never picks. A rise here can be a deal, a deposit, an acquisition, a reclassification, a season or a data slip.

A company is listed if a balance rose at least 25% and $10M in one quarter, to at least $25M, on the same tag, with the two balances 75 to 105 days apart. Dollars only, US GAAP only. Those limits are Claude's first choices, in `sensors/radar/settings.toml`.

What a frame cannot do: it shows one number per company per quarter, last filed. It holds no filing date, so the date beside each figure is the balance date. An earlier figure may have been restated in a later filing. Companies that disclose these only in a 10-K, or only in text, do not appear.
