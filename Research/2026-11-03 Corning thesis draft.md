# Corning thesis, first draft, Nov. 3, 2026

**The business case is plausible. The case for paying $123.20 is not shown.**

Corning sells scarce fiber to AI and telecom buyers on long deals, and that is showing up in sales. On our approved method that is worth **$36.06 a share, 29% of the $123.20 close** (calc., `strategic.html`; close of Nov. 2, 2026, [Yahoo through yfinance, fetched Nov. 2, 2026](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/GLW?range=1d&interval=1d)). ## Six answers

### 1. The mechanism

**Scarcity turns into long deals, deals into plants, and plants into sales.**

1. **Demand outruns supply.** A 180M fiber-km shortfall in 2026 ([TrendForce, Oct. 19, 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261019-13275.html)) and a 150M to 200M gap in 2027 ([Eastmoney, Oct. 20, 2026](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203900316315.html)). Both are forecasts.

2. **Buyers sign long deals.** Meta up to $6 billion ([Meta post, Jan. 27, 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/meta-6-billion-agreement-corning-support-us-manufacturing/)). Four multibillion-dollar deals in Q3, including Verizon and AT&T ([Q3 release, Oct. 27, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000024741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm)).

3. **Some pay up front.** A $1.0 billion deposit ([10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000279/glw-20260930.htm)) and $500M for warrants ([8-K, May 6, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000120677426000273/glw4631061-8k.htm)).

4. **Corning builds.** US fiber capacity up more than 50%, no date given ([Q2 release, July 28, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000024741/000002474126000253/glw-20260728xex99xq22026.htm)).

5. **Sales and profit rise.** Optical sales up 44% ([Q3 release, Oct. 27, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000024741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm)). Optical earned 22.4% on sales, $532M on $2,379M (calc., [10-Q, filed Oct. 29, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000279/glw-20260930.htm)), before corporate costs, and about 60% of segment profit (calc., [Q3 release, Oct. 27, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000024741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm)).

**Weak link:** a deposit is not profit. "We return that cash" as customers buy ([Q3 call, Oct. 27, 2026, third-party transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/27/corning-glw-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/), read through a summarizing fetch).

### 5. What today's price already implies

**21.8% cash growth a year for ten years, to about $10.8 billion in year ten (calc.).**

**Scale:** one such year is more than the last ten together, $8,928M (calc., [10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2019](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474119000016/glw-20181231x10k.htm) to [10-K, filed Feb. 12, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000124/glw-20251231.htm)). It is 27% of $40 billion in sales, or 19% of an assumed $56 billion. The best in the record is 15.0%, in FY2016 (calc., `priced-in.html`).

**Even with the plan fully met** at that best share, it still needs six more years of 8.3% growth, against 5.8% in the record (calc., `priced-in.html`).

**In our yardstick:** with all five true, the price needs about 29x new profit, against 13.3x (calc., `strategic.html`).

**Analysts read it differently:** average target $180, range $132 to $220, 14 Buy, 4 Hold, 0 Sell ([Investing.com GLW page, undated, retrieved Nov. 3, 2026, read through a summarizing fetch](https://uk.investing.com/equities/corning-inc)). Those are opinions and forecasts. Their lowest sits 7% above the close (calc.).

**A softer ask:** let growth fade over five more years and it is 18.4% and $8.2 billion. At a 7% rate it is 15.6% and $6.4 billion (calc., `priced-in.html`). The 9% rate and the 3% growth after year ten are assumptions.
